Frequently asked questions

Who can benefit from medical massage?

Individuals with chronic muscle tension, movement limitations, or those recovering from injuries may benefit. A pre-treatment assessment determines whether medical massage is appropriate and which techniques to use.

Are there any contraindications?

If you have acute infections, fever, suspected thrombosis, certain cardiovascular conditions or are on anticoagulant therapy, consult your physician before treatment. Our therapists review medical history to plan safe care.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about medical massage

Here are the answers to our patients' most common questions.

How does medical massage differ from classical massage?

Medical massage is targeted at a specific musculoskeletal problem and is often part of a broader manual treatment, whereas classical massage is primarily relaxing.

Can a massage be painful?

With pronounced muscle tension the pressure may be noticeably intense, but the force applied is always adjusted to the patient's sensitivity.

Is the massage performed alone or in combination with other therapies?

It is often performed in combination with manual therapy or osteopathy for a better and longer‑lasting effect.

How often is a massage recommended?

Frequency depends on the condition of the muscles and lifestyle - we agree on this during the initial examination.
