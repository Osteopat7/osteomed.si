A different approach from manual therapy

Working with the body's energy, not with physical pressure

While osteopathy and manual therapy act through direct manual pressure on joints and muscles, biotherapy is a much gentler method focused on breathing, relaxation of the nervous system and the body's overall energetic balance. Helpful information

Biotherapy at our spine practice in Maribor

Biotherapy refers to medical approaches that use biological mechanisms or products to support the body's healing processes. At our spine practice in Maribor, a individual assessment assesses whether biotherapy is appropriate based on clinical evaluation and diagnostic results. We provide a clear treatment plan, informed consent discussion, and follow-up to monitor response and safety.

Frequently asked questions

What is biotherapy?

Biotherapy involves medical treatments that harness biological agents or mechanisms to support healing. Specific methods and suitability are determined by a specialist after clinical assessment.

Who is a candidate for biotherapy?

Eligibility depends on the underlying condition, patient history, and diagnostic findings. A clinician evaluates potential benefits and risks before recommending biotherapy.

What should I expect during treatment?

Expect an initial consultation, tailored treatment plan, administration of the therapy as indicated, and scheduled follow-up visits to assess response and manage any issues.

Are there side effects?

Side effects vary by type of biotherapy and individual response. We discuss possible adverse effects prior to starting treatment and provide guidance on monitoring and when to seek medical attention.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about biotherapy

Here are the answers to our patients' most common questions.

How does biotherapy differ from osteopathy or manual therapy?

Osteopathy and manual therapy work through direct manual pressure on joints and muscles. Biotherapy is much gentler and is based on breathing and energetic balance, without physical manipulations.

Does biotherapy replace medical treatment?

No. Biotherapy is a complementary method and does not replace diagnostics or treatment by a medical specialist.

Who is biotherapy suitable for?

Primarily for people experiencing stress, inner tension or feelings of exhaustion and seeking gentle complementary support.

How often is it recommended to come?

Frequency is individual and arranged according to the patient's condition and needs.
