When to consult an osteopath

Signs that it's time to visit

The osteopath in Maribor, Dr. Sergey Verbitskiy, treats a wide range of musculoskeletal problems. Osteopathy and manual therapy are appropriate at the first signs of tension, not only when pain becomes chronic. Common reasons patients seek assessment include:

  • 🔹 Recurring lower back pain, that do not go away with rest
  • 🔹 Pain radiating into the leg or arm (suspected sciatica or disc herniation)
  • 🔹 Neck stiffness and limited mobility when turning the head
  • 🔹 Headaches related to tension in the cervical spine
  • 🔹 Pain in the shoulders or knees, which hinder daily activities
  • 🔹 Feeling of a "block" after an awkward movement, lifting loads or prolonged sitting

If you recognize any of the signs listed, early treatment usually leads to faster improvement. For persistent or severe pain, loss of sensation in the limbs, or sudden muscle weakness, always consult a doctor first, as the condition may require further diagnostic assessment.

First visit

What to expect at your first appointment

The initial visit with the osteopath is designed for Dr. Verbitskiy to obtain a comprehensive picture of your health, lifestyle and movement patterns. The assessment typically includes a discussion of your symptoms and their onset, a clinical evaluation of posture, spinal and joint mobility, and palpation of muscle tone. Based on the findings, the first manual treatment follows, tailored to your tolerance and condition.

No referral is required for the visit - you can book an appointment directly by phone or WhatsApp. We recommend comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the spine, neck and the joints being treated. After the first treatment you will receive recommendations for next steps, including the likely number of visits needed.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about therapy

Here are the answers to our patients' most common questions.

What is manual therapy?

Manual therapy is a specialized treatment method that uses hands-on techniques to remove restrictions in joints and muscles, reduce pain and improve mobility.

Is osteopathy safe?

Yes, manual therapy with Dr. Verbitskiy is completely safe, as aggressive chiropractic manipulations are not performed. Each treatment is tailored to the patient's age and condition.

How many treatments are needed?

The number of visits depends on the problem. Many patients feel relief after the first treatment; for chronic issues, usually 3 to 5 visits are needed.

What conditions is it suitable for?

Therapy is effective for back, neck and lower back pain, herniated disc, sciatica, headaches and limited joint mobility.

Do I need a referral to visit an osteopath?

No, a referral is not required. You can book an appointment with Dr. Verbitskiy in Maribor directly by phone or WhatsApp, without public healthcare waiting times.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Appointments are usually available within a few days. For acute pain we endeavour to find the earliest available slot.

Do you also treat sports injuries?

Yes. Manual therapy and rehabilitation are suitable for sports injuries to muscles, tendons and joints, with a gradual return to full activity.
